## 5. Review Notes（英語・App Review Information → Notes・全文上書き）

```
Fast News AI shows a swipeable feed of short generative-AI news summaries in Japanese and English. No account. No in-app purchase.

How to review:
1. Fresh install and open the app (allow network for the public feed JSON).
2. Home: swipe vertical cards. Top tabs: For you / By source / By tool.
3. Explore: tap a grid item to open card view; use “back to explore”.
4. Settings: change filters (sources × genres × tools) and language.
5. Optional: tap the source icon/name on a card to jump to that source feed.

Privacy: preferences, saved articles, and “not interested” IDs are stored on-device only. The app fetches a public HTTPS feed. No tracking / no ATT prompt.
Privacy Policy: https://soroa-studio.com/ainews/privacy.html
Terms: https://soroa-studio.com/ainews/terms.html
Support: https://soroa-studio.com/ainews/ or support@soroa-studio.com

Sponsored cards (if shown) are labeled as ads (広告). No third-party ad network or click-tracking SDK.
```

**Contact:** 届く氏名・電話・メールを ASC に登録。  
**Demo account:** 不要（ログインなし）。
